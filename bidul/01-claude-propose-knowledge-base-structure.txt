Use the `knowledge-base-architect` skill.

Analyze the existing KnowledgeBase and the entire repository before making any changes.

Goals:
- Preserve all useful existing knowledge.
- Reorganize the KnowledgeBase around clear topics and responsibilities.
- Keep the structure simple and shallow.
- Use README.md files as navigation hubs.
- Connect related topics with internal Markdown links.
- Remove arbitrary numbered document ordering such as 01-, 02-, 03- unless sequence is genuinely required.
- Avoid duplicated information. Each fact should have one canonical source of truth.
- Separate verified facts, known risks, and open questions.
- Do not invent facts that cannot be verified from the repository.
- Make the KnowledgeBase easy for both developers and AI agents to navigate with minimal context.
- Prefer focused documents instead of large catch-all files.
- Identify orphan documents, duplicated content, conflicting information, missing documentation, and broken or outdated links.
- Keep the current technical content unless it is duplicated, outdated, or belongs in a different canonical topic.

Before proposing the new structure:
1. Read the current KnowledgeBase.
2. Inspect the repository structure.
3. Identify the real applications, libraries, infrastructure components, external integrations, configuration areas, CI/CD, testing, operations, observability, APIs, workflows, and important failure modes.
4. Build an inventory of the existing KnowledgeBase content.
5. Map every existing document or important section to its proposed destination.
6. Identify duplicates and unclear/uncertain information.

Then show me ONLY a proposed restructuring plan.

The proposal must include:
- The complete proposed KnowledgeBase directory tree.
- A short explanation of the purpose of every top-level topic.
- Which current files/content will move into each new topic.
- Which files should be merged, split, renamed, or removed because they are duplicates.
- Which information should become the canonical source of truth.
- Any open questions or gaps discovered.
- Any risks of losing context during migration.
- A short explanation of why the new structure is simpler and easier to navigate.

Important:
- DO NOT modify, move, rename, create, or delete any files yet.
- DO NOT rewrite the current KnowledgeBase yet.
- DO NOT apply the migration yet.
- Wait for my explicit approval of the proposed structure.
